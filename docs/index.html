The core idea

A model can predict an action correctly while its planning objective rejects the route to success.

Same predictor. Different target.

Use recorded experience to choose where to aim, then predict actions from the current state.

01 / The insight

The shortest-looking step
can miss the route.

Reaching a goal may require first moving away from it. A short-horizon planner that minimizes final-goal distance can reject exactly those actions.

Our counterexample makes this distinction explicit: even with exact dynamics and globally optimal short-horizon search, final-goal scoring can stall. Successive observed targets along the same feasible route enable progress.

02 / Anchored Planning

Experience tells us where to aim.
Prediction tells us how to approach it.

A recorded segment connects a nearby observation to a distant outcome. Anchored Planning retrieves that segment and uses its short-term observed successor as the planning target.

  1. Retrieve a routeMatch the current image to a recorded start, and the goal image to the segment’s distant endpoint.
  2. Aim at an observationUse the observation five actions after the retrieved start as the default target.
  3. Predict from hereThe frozen world model evaluates actions from the live state. The same target supports continuous action synthesis and selection among recorded actions.

03 / Evidence

More control from the same predictor.

Across Cube, PushT, Reacher, and TwoRoom, changing the target improves both action synthesis and recorded-action ranking with task-specific frozen LeWM models.

Continuous action synthesis · mean success

9.2% → 59.8%

Final-goal CEM → observed-target AP-CEM

Recorded-action ranking · mean success

67.0% → 83.8%

Learned and observed targets both improve on final-goal scoring. Observed targets give the higher mean for action synthesis; learned targets perform best on Cube and TwoRoom. The paper reports all controllers, perturbations, and target-quality comparisons.

The gap grows beyond the shortest goal offsets.

Searching harder does not recover the same gains.

Observed-target CEM with two iterations exceeds thirty-iteration final-goal CEM on every task in the measured standard-start budget sweep.

04 / A recorded episode

A target from experience.
An action chosen for the present.
